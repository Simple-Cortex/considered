# Read mode

Use when the human must learn, retrieve, comprehend, or apply knowledge. The surface succeeds when the reader can find the idea, understand its evidence, and return to it later. It is not a collection of decorative content modules.

The `READ-` rules below are guidance rules: no linter emits them, and they surface only by reading this catalog at its routed stage.

## Frame

State the proposition or instructional outcome the reader should retain. Identify the reader's starting knowledge, the decision or application that follows, and whether they read sequentially, scan for an answer, or revisit as reference. Label unknown expertise or reading environment as assumptions unless it changes accessibility, legal meaning, or the primary learning outcome.

## Structure

- `READ-01` (current idea outranks chrome): let P0 be the current proposition, instruction, or answer, not the page title or navigation.
- Use P1 for the argument steps, key example, definitions needed now, and the next instructional action.
- Use P2 for supporting evidence, alternative examples, references, and contextual detail.
- Put long reference material, glossary depth, source lists, and appendix detail at P3.
- Keep table of contents, progress, search, sharing, and utility actions at P4.
- Order zones as claim or question, explanation, evidence or example, then application or next step.
- `READ-02` (headings do argumentative work): use headings that advance the argument. Avoid card grids that fragment a line of thought.

## Compose and harden

`READ-03` (stable measure and explicit location): preserve a stable reading measure, semantic heading order, visible focus, readable contrast, and an explicit location in long content. `READ-04` (definitions at the point of need): make definitions available where comprehension needs them. Respect sequential reading while letting a scanner recover context and return to the exact location.

For long or dynamic content, `READ-06` (long-content states specified) requires loading, missing sources, outdated guidance, permission-limited sections, broken references, print behavior, copy behavior, and narrow-width reflow. `READ-05` (no invented sources or certainty): do not invent citations, certainty, endorsements, or results.

`READ-07` (retrieval genre outranks the direction): a reading surface used for retrieval — documentation, reference, changelogs — follows its genre before it follows the dealt direction: neutral high-contrast ground, persistent page identity, navigation and search that survive scrolling, and density tuned for scanning rather than for savoring. An editorial re-theme of a docs page slows the reader it exists to serve; blind review against shipped documentation ranked exactly that failure last. Express the direction in accents, code-block treatment, and details — never as a re-theme of the genre.

## Review

Check that surrounding chrome does not outrank the current idea, each heading earns its place in the argument, actions are quiet and contextual, and the reader can state what changed in their understanding. Name `Content Fog` when decoration or fragmentation breaks continuity.
